Questions ski school directors ask.

Can parents book and pay for lessons online?

Yes. Your booking page lets guests request a lesson and pay by card through Stripe, in English or Spanish, and check where their request stands.

Can we sell lesson packages?

Yes. Packages count down as lessons are used, and each guest's lessons show on their customer record.

How do instructors share when they can work?

Instructors post their own open dates, and those dates count when you schedule. You can also show them on an optional public page.

What is the refund policy for online lessons?

You set it in steps: a full refund up to one point, a partial refund after that, then none.

Does it handle camps and race programs?

Yes. Clubs and programs runs camps, leagues and recurring sessions, with rosters and attendance.

Do families need a waiver for lessons?

Ski school shows whether each guest has a waiver that counts, and parents can sign for their children at home before they arrive.
