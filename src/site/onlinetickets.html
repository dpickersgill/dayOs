Questions marketing and ticketing managers ask.

Does Verdynt/OS take a cut of each ticket?

No. Card payments go into your own Stripe account, and Stripe charges its usual card fees to that account. Pricing shows exactly what your plan costs.

Can we limit how many tickets sell for a busy day?

Yes. Set a daily limit, and when a day sells out guests can join a waitlist.

Can guests change their date?

Yes, online, for a fee you set and up to a number of hours before the day that you choose.

Can we sell season passes online?

Yes, with the pass holder's photo taken during checkout.

Does it work on the website we already have?

Yes. The shop has its own page with your logo and colors. Link to it from your website's Buy tickets button.
