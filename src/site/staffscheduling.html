Questions department heads ask.

How does it know how many people we need?

It looks at the tickets already sold for the day and the season passes you expect, and suggests staffing for the day next to the schedule.

How do staff see their shifts?

When you publish the week, each person gets their shifts in their Inbox, which they can open on their own phone.

How does clocking in work?

A tablet at the staff entrance runs the time clock. Staff clock in and out with a PIN or a badge, and the hours and overtime are ready for payroll.

Does it track certifications?

Yes. Each certification has an expiry date, and the ones that run out soon are flagged.

What about ski patrol schedules?

Ski patrol has its own schedule, cover requests and volunteer days in the Patrol app, built for paid and volunteer patrols.
