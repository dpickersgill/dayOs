The Good Day

For ski area owners, GMs and patrol directors. One moment of your guest's day, taken apart: the question in their head, the process behind the answer, one number to watch and a quick test.

What it is

A good day, designed

A guest's day on your mountain is six moments. Each one has a question in the guest's head and a process behind the answer. When the process is written down and the system holds it, the day works the same on a quiet Tuesday as on a powder Saturday.

1. Plan
Can I trust what I'm about to buy?
2. Arrive
Where do I go first, and how long will it take?
3. Learn
Is my kid in good hands?
4. Ride
Is it open, and is it safe?
5. Get help
If something goes wrong, will someone come?
6. Come back
Do they remember me?

Who it is for

The people who run the day

  • Owners and GMswho want the busy days to run the same way every time, without being there for every decision.
  • Patrol directorswho want the rules decided before the season, written down, and followed on the hill.
  • Anyone at an independent ski areawho has ever said "only Sue knows how that works".

Each issue takes about five minutes to read. No vendor gossip and no sales pitch: one moment, what usually breaks, and something you can try this week.
