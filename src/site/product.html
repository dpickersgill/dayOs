Patient care

One record from the first call to the take-home sheet.

Patient details are limited to the patrol access levels you allow.
Ski patrol software
What it does
  • Dispatch assigns responders, alerts their phones and keeps the call timeline
  • A case page in three views: Care on the hill, Report for the write-up, Record for the history
  • Vitals with age-based flags, GCS, primary survey, head, spine and stroke checks
  • Splints with circulation checks before and after, tourniquet and pelvic binder times, oxygen, and a cardiac arrest log with a running clock
  • Medications only from your formulary, by who may give them, taken from stock by lot
  • Keeps working without signal and sends in order when it returns
  • The report fills itself from the bedside, with a CHART or SOAP draft narrative to correct
  • Refusal of care and the hand-off to EMS signed on the phone, and a one-page hand-off sheet that prints
  • Nothing is deleted: mistakes are marked entered in error, and each submitted report is kept as a version
  • Collision forms, take-home sheets, follow-up and giving, and a review group that signs off reports

Patrol operations

The patrol day around the cases.

The mountain cannot be called clear while a route is open or someone found has no call.
What it does
  • On duty: sign in and out with station and radio, on a phone or the patrol-room tablet
  • Phone alerts for calls, sweep routes and search areas, with "On my way" to answer
  • Opening checks for every run, and holds and closures with a reason
  • The closing sweep: routes assigned to whoever is on duty, stop by stop, with a late alert
  • Lift evacuation plans and drills, and who on duty is ready for each lift
  • Oxygen pressure and AED checks, with pads and battery dates
  • Avalanche paths, observations and control shots, off unless you need them
  • A daily log built from the day, with addenda that never overwrite it
  • Records requests released only from the approved report, with a legal hold that stops changes
  • Lost-person searches with areas assigned and alerted
