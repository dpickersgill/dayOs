Verdynt/OS is operations software for independent ski areas: family-owned hills, community and municipal areas, and nonprofit mountains. It is made by {{COMPANY}}, in {{PLACE}}.

Who we are

{{COMPANY}} builds and runs the software that operations depend on: the systems a business opens with every morning, and the steps people follow around them. Verdynt/OS is our product for small and mid-size mountains. We build it for crews that are part paid, part seasonal and often part volunteer, like many ski patrols. A volunteer crew can do a lot with the right tools. It has little patience for the wrong ones.

Why we built it

The large resort groups buy enterprise systems with teams to run them. Most independent ski areas can't, so they run on a patchwork: an old ticketing system, a card terminal that doesn't talk to it, patrol reports on paper, lift checks on a clipboard and a schedule in a spreadsheet. Every piece works on its own. Together they cost a small crew hours every week, and leave records that are hard to find when they matter most.

We built Verdynt/OS to put those pieces in one place, priced for a small hill, so each mountain turns on only the parts it runs.

What we believe

  • Safety records come first. Patient care reports are never deleted, a lift that fails its inspection cannot be opened, and a park feature that fails its inspection stays closed until an inspection reopens it.
  • Your money is yours. Guest card payments go straight into each mountain's own Stripe account. We never hold it.
  • Your data is yours. Every mountain can export its records at any time. Details are in our Terms and Privacy policy.
  • A seasonal crew should learn it in a morning. It runs in a web browser on the computers, tablets and phones a mountain already has.
  • Saturday morning matters most. We release changes on Sunday nights, never on a weekend morning, and a problem at the ticket window or the gate is answered first.

How we work with mountains

  • Try it first. The demo opens on your own mountain's lifts and trails in about 20 seconds, and a 14-day trial mountain needs no card.
  • No long contracts. Monthly, yearly or over the season, and every mountain can leave with its data.
  • Built with the people who use it. Founding mountains pay half in their first season and help decide what we build next.
