How it works on a busy Saturday.

From the tickets sold the night before to the drawer counted at close.

  1. The night before, the shop sells to its limit.Guests buy named tickets for Saturday on the online shop. The day's limit counts window sales too, and when it is reached a waitlist opens.
  2. Online buyers skip the window.Their tickets carry a barcode and a QR code. A guest who cannot find theirs is found at will-call by email, phone or name.
  3. The window moves fast.Day tickets open on the quick sale, with no details asked for. A second phone walks the line in Line mode. A school group is one sale with the roster pasted in.
  4. Problems are caught before payment.A guest with no waiver that counts, or a day at its ticket limit, is flagged on the details step, never after the card is charged. Only a manager can sell past a limit, with a reason.
  5. The gate checks every scan the same way.A barcode, an RFID card tap or a paired turnstile all go through one check: valid dates, rides left, voided, blocked cards and the pass-back hold. The result shows the guest type in large letters, so an adult on a child's ticket stands out.
  6. A lost card stops working at once.Press Lost on the card and it is refused at every gate. The pass moves to a new card, and only a manager can unblock the old one.
  7. Plans change, tickets move.An unused dated ticket moves to another day at the window and keeps its code.
  8. At close, the money adds up.Each cashier counts their drawer blind and sees what is over or short. Unusual scans, such as one pass at two lifts too far apart, wait for a manager to review.

Questions about tickets and passes.

Can we use RFID cards?

Yes. Load a ticket or pass on a card at the counter, tap it at the gate, and load it again next season. A desk reader that types like a keyboard works in every card field.

Can guests buy lift tickets online?

Yes, with the Online shop add-on. Guests pick a day on a calendar showing that day's lowest price, then buy by card through Stripe. You can set a daily limit, and a waitlist opens when a day sells out.

How do you stop pass sharing?

The gate stops a pass that was just used, and scan history shows when and where each pass was scanned. Season passes sold online can carry the guest's photo.

Do refunds need approval?

Every refund needs a reason, and the reason is kept with the sale. Large refunds ask the person to confirm who they are again.

Can we sell day tickets without taking every guest's details?

Yes. A basket of day tickets, punch cards or gift cards opens on the quick sale, with no name asked for. Season passes, lessons and rentals always need the person's details. A manager can turn quick sale off.

Do the gates keep working if the network drops?

Paired gates and turnstiles keep a list of today's good cards and ticket codes, decide on their own, and send their scans when the network is back. Each scan is saved once, at the time it happened.

How do card payments work?

Guests pay into your own Stripe account, and Stripe pays it out to your bank. Card numbers never touch Verdynt/OS, and Verdynt/OS never holds your money. Stripe charges its card fees to your account, and Verdynt/OS adds nothing on top.
