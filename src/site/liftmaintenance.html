Questions lift and maintenance managers ask.

Can a lift open without an inspection?

No. A lift that fails its inspection cannot be set to open until a passing check is on file.

Can operators do inspections on a phone?

Yes. Inspection checklists run on a phone, and wind holds and downtime are logged as they happen.

How do pre-shift checks on groomers work?

Scan the QR tag on a groomer, truck or snowmobile to open its pre-use checklist. A failed item opens a work order, marked urgent on critical equipment.

Can service be scheduled by engine hours?

Yes. Service plans run by days, hours or miles, and are marked done when their work order is completed.

Does it track parts and costs?

Yes, with the Assets and fleet add-on: parts with bins and reorder points, purchase orders, and labor, parts, outside work and fuel per machine. Only managers see costs.

Do we need Assets and fleet for basic work orders?

No. Basic work orders stay in Lifts and trails for everyone. Assets and fleet adds the full register, service plans, parts and costs.
