Questions ticket office managers ask.

Which card reader does it use?

A Stripe smart reader, such as the Stripe Reader S700. You pair it once under Billing, then choose Charge on reader at checkout, and the reader shows the exact total.

Where does the card money go?

Straight into your mountain's own Stripe account, on Stripe's usual payout schedule. Verdynt/OS never holds your money.

Can we price adults, children and seniors differently?

Yes. Every product can have its own price for each guest type, and you can add your own types, such as College, Local or Military, and change their names and order.

Does it do gift cards?

Yes. Sell gift cards at the counter or online and take them as payment, with each card's balance kept.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. The demo builds a sample mountain in a minute with sales already in it, and every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with no card needed.
