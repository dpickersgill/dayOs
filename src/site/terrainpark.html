Questions park managers ask.

How accurate is the GPS pin?

As accurate as the phone can manage where you stand. The screen shows the accuracy in feet as it improves and drops the pin once the fix is good, or you can drop it straight away and drag it into place.

Do we have to place every feature every day?

No. Start from an earlier day's layout and move only what changed.

What happens when a feature fails inspection?

The inspector adds a note, and the feature's status changes everywhere it shows, so nobody opens it by mistake.

Can someone change a past day's record?

Only a manager. Past days are kept as a record of what was in the park and what was checked.

Does it need an app from the app store?

No. It runs in the phone's web browser, with nothing to install.
