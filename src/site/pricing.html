Pricing questions.

Is there a contract?

No. Monthly billing is month to month, yearly billing is paid once a year, and season billing is six payments from November to April. Cancel from the Billing page and you keep everything until the end of the period you paid for.

What is paying over the season?

The yearly price, split into six monthly payments from November to April, so you pay while the lifts are turning, and nothing in the months between. It costs the same over the year as paying yearly, and everything keeps working all year. Sign up between May and October and the first payment is November 1.

We're open in summer too. Which plan?

Attractions are part of Lifts and trails, which every plan includes. Selling activities is part of Sell.

What happens when the trial ends?

If you have chosen a plan and a way to pay, nothing changes. If not, everything keeps working for 14 more days with a note at the top of every page, then Verdynt/OS becomes read only until you pay.

What does read only mean?

Everyone can still sign in, see and export everything. Nothing new is sold, booked or added, and the online shop stops taking orders. Patrol, the time clock and the team Inbox keep working, because care records and hours should never wait on a bill. Paying turns everything back on straight away.

Can we buy just a few apps?

Start with the plan nearest to what you run and add what you need. If none fits, tell us what you run and we will price it with you.

What does Assistant Plus add?

Every plan includes Ask, which answers how-to questions from Help and your setup. Assistant Plus adds questions about your own day (scans, sales, lessons, program days, who is working), limited to what each person's role can already see, and a Questions button for guests on the online pages you choose. A monthly allowance is built in, so the bill never surprises you. The assistant never sees patient care records.

Do you take a cut of ticket sales?

No. The price is the same however much you sell online. Guests pay into your own Stripe account, and the only fees on a sale are the card processor's.

Why is Resort a better deal than adding apps to Mountain?

Resort includes Patrol, the Online shop, Terrain park, Assets and fleet, and Messages. Added to Mountain one by one, those cost more each month than Resort does.

Can we change plans or switch apps on and off?

Yes. The owner changes the plan and add-ons on the Billing page, and the apps change to match. Turning an app off hides it from your crew without deleting anything.

What happens to our data if we leave?

It is yours. Customers, sales, payments, refunds and incident reports download as spreadsheets whenever you want them.
