Pricing questions.

Is there a contract?

No. Monthly billing is month to month, and yearly billing is paid once a year. Cancel from the Billing page and you keep everything until the end of the period you paid for.

What happens when the trial ends?

If you have chosen a plan and a way to pay, nothing changes. If not, everything keeps working for 14 more days with a note at the top of every page, then SummitOps becomes read only until you pay.

What does read only mean?

Everyone can still sign in, see and export everything. Nothing new is sold, booked or added, and the online shop stops taking orders. Patrol, the time clock and the team Inbox keep working, because care records and hours should never wait on a bill. Paying turns everything back on straight away.

Can we buy just a few apps?

Start with the plan nearest to what you run and add what you need. If none fits, tell us what you run and we will price it with you.

We need Patrol but not the bigger plan.

Patrol is an add-on for Lodge and Mountain, and included in Resort. Many volunteer patrols start there.

Can we change plans or switch apps on and off?

Yes. The owner changes the plan and add-ons on the Billing page, and the apps change to match. Turning an app off hides it from your crew without deleting anything.

What happens to our data if we leave?

It is yours. Customers, sales, payments, refunds and incident reports download as spreadsheets whenever you want them.
