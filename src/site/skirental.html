How it works on a busy Saturday.

From the group bags packed the night before to the last tag scanned back in. A one-counter shop skips the stations and fits from one card.

  1. The night before, groups are set aside.For a school or club group, parents filled in each child's rental form from the leader's link. The shop sets gear aside for each member, records the binding setting and test, and prints a label for each bag.
  2. Guests fill in their forms in the car.Anyone who bought a rental gets a link and a QR code on the receipt. They enter height, weight, boot size and how they ski, and sign that it is true. Walk-ins scan the QR poster in line.
  3. Paid rentals wait in Ready to fit.Rentals sold at the ticket window or online wait for the shop. The counter scans the receipt and everyone on that order opens together.
  4. Each station works its own queue.A shop with stations moves each person through Form, Boots, Ski tech and Door, oldest first. A queue board on a wall screen shows first names and an initial only.
  5. The ski tech works from the fitting card.The card shows the setting your chart suggests from today's details, never from a past rental. The technician records the setting, who set it and the test result. A failed test cannot be rented out.
  6. The deposit goes on the guest's card.With card deposits switched on, the deposit is held on the guest's card at the reader after hand out. Nothing is charged unless fees are owed, and the hold is let go when everything is back. If the reader is offline, the counter takes a note deposit instead.
  7. The group leaves with one scan per bag.A bag without a waiver or signed details stays behind, and the screen says why.
  8. Returns move as fast as the scanner.Each tag scanned on the Returns tab is checked in. Damaged gear goes to service, lost gear starts at what it cost the shop, and a late fee follows the mountain's own clock. When messages are set up, a renter whose gear is an hour overdue gets an automatic reminder.

Questions rental managers ask.

Does it work with barcodes?

Yes. Each piece of gear gets a barcode tag. Scan it to add it to the cart, and scan the receipt at pickup.

Do you keep binding setting records?

Yes. Each binding setting is recorded with the technician and the test result, and every item keeps a service log.

Can we rent to a whole group on one contract?

Yes. Group contracts put everyone on one contract, with a signed waiver for everyone wearing gear.

How are late and damaged returns handled?

Overdue gear is flagged, and late, damage and loss fees can be collected or waived, with a record either way.

Does Verdynt/OS come with a binding chart?

No, on purpose. Your shop enters the chart it uses, and the technician always records the setting they actually set and the test result. The suggestion comes from today's details, never from a past rental.

Can we rent gear for the whole season?

Yes. A season rental goes home until your return date, the renter gets a reminder in March, and a manager can sell them the gear at the end with Buy out.

Can guests sign the rental waiver before they arrive?

Yes. They sign on their phone at home, on a counter tablet, or from an emailed link, and Rentals shows whether each guest has a waiver that counts.
