Questions about online waivers.

Can a parent sign for their children?

Yes. A parent or guardian signs for the children in one go, and gets a copy of exactly what they signed.

What happens when we change our waiver wording?

Every version of the wording is kept, and you can ask everyone to sign again when the wording changes.

Do guests get a copy?

Yes. Each signer gets a private copy of exactly what they signed.

Can guests sign at the counter?

Yes. A counter tablet mode clears after each family, so the next family starts fresh.

Is the waiver checked when we sell a ticket or rental?

Yes. Sell, Rentals, Ski school and the Gate all show whether a guest has a waiver that counts.

Do waivers cost extra?

No. Waivers come with every Verdynt/OS plan.
