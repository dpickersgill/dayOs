How it works on a busy Saturday.

Most signatures happen before the guest arrives. The rest happen at the counter without holding up the line.

  1. The night before, families sign at home.Your signing page at /waiver/your-mountain works on any phone, from a link on your website or a QR code. One adult signs for themselves and up to 10 children, and draws the signature with a finger.
  2. Each signer gets their own copy.A private link shows exactly what they signed, with a button to print or save it as a PDF. It is emailed only to the signer's own address.
  3. Online buyers are covered as they pay.A guest buying tickets in the online shop signs for themselves and their children before paying, and the waiver is recorded when the payment lands.
  4. At the counter, a tablet does the rest.Counter tablet mode shows the same signing page and clears itself after each family. Staff can also turn their own screen round with Sign on this screen, or send Email a link to a guest still in the car.
  5. Every desk sees who is covered.Sell, Rentals, Ski school and the Gate show whether each guest has a waiver that counts. A missing one is caught before the sale, not at the lift or the rental door.
  6. Children stay with their own family.Only an adult signs, and a child who already has a different guardian on file is never signed for by someone else. Online, that signing is set aside as a possible duplicate for staff to check.
  7. Lessons and groups sign the same way.The ski school desk records which parent or guardian signed for a student under 18, and each child in a school group gets a waiver link a parent signs.
  8. After close, the register is complete.Search everyone signed this season, open any waiver to see the wording, signature, typed name, time and device, and print it when an insurer or attorney asks. Managers void a mistake with a reason and download the season as a CSV.

Questions about online waivers.

Can a parent sign for their children?

Yes. An adult signs for themselves and up to 10 children in one go, and gets a copy of exactly what they signed. A child who already has a different guardian on file is never signed for by someone else.

What happens when we change our waiver wording?

Every version of the wording is kept word for word, so an older waiver always shows what that guest read. When a change matters, Ask everyone to sign again makes earlier waivers stop counting, and guests sign the new wording.

Do guests get a copy?

Yes. Each signer gets a private copy of exactly what they signed.

Can guests sign at the counter?

Yes. A counter tablet mode clears after each family, so the next family starts fresh.

Is the waiver checked when we sell a ticket or rental?

Yes. Sell, Rentals, Ski school and the Gate all show whether a guest has a waiver that counts.

Do you provide the waiver wording?

There is a sample to start from, but it is not legal advice. Your wording is yours: have your insurer and attorney review it before guests sign.

Do waivers cost extra?

No. Waivers come with every Verdynt/OS plan.
